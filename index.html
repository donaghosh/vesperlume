The hour before the light goes.

Portrait and wedding photography, Bengaluru.

A small team working in available light.

We photograph what is already happening.

Vesperlume is a small team of photographers who have spent years learning to disappear into a room. We work in whatever light is already there — a window, a doorway, the last half hour of sun — and we don't ask anyone to stand anywhere.

Between us we've photographed more than four hundred weddings, and the thing we keep relearning is that the picture people frame is almost never the one they asked for. So we watch for it instead.

Portraits are covered by one photographer. Weddings bring two, sometimes three. Film on request.
